Lakeside Tent Pitches

Level, grassy sites right on the water's edge — bring your own canvas, and we'll bring the firewood, hot showers and the loons calling at dusk.

Our simplest, wildest way to stay: a marked-out pitch on the shoreline, your own tent, and the whole lake for a front garden. Every site gets its own firepit and enough space to spread out.

Pitch by the water

Sites are generous, flat and mostly grass, with a mix of open shoreline spots and sheltered pitches back in the pines. Firewood is stacked and dry at the gatehouse, and the washblock — proper hot showers included — is never more than a short walk away.

  • Level grass pitch for a tent up to 6 metres
  • Private firepit with a grill grate and dry firewood nearby
  • Shoreline or sheltered woodland spots to choose from
  • Hot showers, flushing toilets and washing-up sinks
  • Drinking-water taps and a small camp shop on site
  • Dark, low-light skies — some of the best stargazing around

Good to know

Cars can drop gear at the pitch, then park in the meadow lot. Dogs are welcome on a lead, quiet hours start at 10pm, and campfires are always allowed in the firepits — even in high summer.
