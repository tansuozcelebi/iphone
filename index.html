From arc to finish

Welded by hand. Finished like furniture.

Our welders lay every seam by eye and every tank is leak-tested before it leaves the bay. Then it moves to the paint line for a chip-resistant powder-coat in the colour you choose.

  • 01
    Design & CAD

    Your drawing or ours — baffles, ports and mounts modelled first.

  • 02
    Cut & weld

    Laser-cut plate, jig-assembled and full-seam MIG welded.

  • 03
    Test & coat

    Pressure & leak test, then a flawless powder-coat finish.
