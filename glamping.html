Bell Tents & Safari Lodges

Canvas already pitched, beds already made — real mattresses, a wood stove and lantern light. All the camping, with the hard part done.

Turn up with a bag, not a boot-load of gear. Our canvas bell tents and larger safari lodges come made-up and ready — you just kick off your boots and light the stove.

Camping, softened

Each tent sits on its own timber deck with a couple of deckchairs and a firepit facing the water. Inside there's a proper double bed with warm bedding, a wood-burning stove, rugs underfoot and a lantern for when the sky goes dark. Safari lodges add a second sleeping area for families.

  • Made-up double bed with duvets and extra blankets
  • Wood-burning stove and a starter basket of logs
  • Private deck, deckchairs and a lake-facing firepit
  • Solar lantern lighting and USB charging
  • Safari lodges sleep up to four with a family bunk area
  • Shared washblock with hot showers a short stroll away

Good to know

Check-in from 3pm, and we'll have the stove laid ready to light. Bring a torch and warm socks for the evenings; everything else — bedding, kettle, crockery — is already in the tent.
