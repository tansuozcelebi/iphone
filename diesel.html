Diesel Storage Tanks

Baffled, bunded steel reservoirs from 200 L to 10,000 L — built to feed gensets, plant and refuelling rigs without a drop out of place.

Every diesel tank leaves our floor pressure-tested, powder-coated and ready to plumb in. We build to your fuel volume, footprint and mounting — single-wall or fully bunded for spill containment.

Built for the field

Internal baffles keep fuel from surging on the move, and a sloped sump with a low drain makes servicing clean and simple. Lockable fill points, breathers and level senders come fitted to your spec.

  • Laser-cut mild or stainless steel plate, full-seam MIG welded
  • Integrated 110% bund option for spill compliance
  • Anti-surge baffles and sloped sump drain
  • Lockable fill cap, breather and level gauge
  • Chip-resistant powder-coat in any RAL colour
  • Fork pockets or lifting lugs for easy placement

Where they run

Standby generators, construction sites, farms and mobile refuelling. If it burns diesel, we've built the tank that feeds it.
