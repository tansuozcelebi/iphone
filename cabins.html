Timber Cabins in the Pines

Snug off-grid cabins tucked into the treeline — a porch to sit out on, a firepit below and a lake view winking between the pines.

Four walls and a roof when the weather turns, without losing the wild. Our cabins are hand-built from local timber, run on solar, and sit just far enough apart that all you'll hear is the wind in the trees.

A roof in the wild

Inside is warm and simple: a proper bed, a little kitchenette, a wood stove and big windows facing the water. The covered porch is the best seat in the house — coffee at sunrise, a book in the afternoon, the fire crackling below by dark.

  • Hand-built local timber cabin, sleeps two to four
  • Real bed, wood-burning stove and cosy throws
  • Kitchenette with a stove, sink and the basics to cook
  • Covered porch and a private firepit facing the lake
  • Solar power, USB charging and gentle warm lighting
  • Composting toilet in-cabin; hot showers a short walk away

Good to know

Check-in from 3pm. Cabins are off-grid on solar, so pack for a slow, low-power night — that's rather the point. Well-behaved dogs are welcome, and firewood for the stove and pit is included.
