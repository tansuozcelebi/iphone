Mobile Hydraulic Tanks

Vibration-rated, saddle-mounted reservoirs for trucks, tippers and mobile plant — light enough to spare payload, tough enough for the road.

Mobile builds are all about the mount. We shape each tank to your chassis rail or sub-frame, keep the weight down and make sure the oil stays cool and de-aerated however rough the ride gets.

Made to move

Suction and return are baffle-separated so the pump always draws clean, aerated-free oil. Rubber-isolated mounts absorb road shock, and every port is placed for a tidy hose run.

  • Chassis- or sub-frame-matched saddle mounts
  • Aluminium or steel to balance weight and durability
  • Baffled suction/return with de-aeration zone
  • Integrated suction strainer and magnetic drain
  • Sight-glass and temperature gauge fitted
  • Anti-vibration rubber isolation mounts

Where they run

Tipper hoists, crane trucks, hook-lifts, sweepers and agricultural rigs — anywhere hydraulics have to travel.
