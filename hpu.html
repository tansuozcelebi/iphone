HPU Power-Unit Tanks

L-shaped and overhead reservoirs sized to your pump and cooling load — machined ports, clean returns and a drop-in-ready base plate.

The reservoir is the heart of a power unit. We build the tank around your pump, motor and cooler so the whole HPU bolts together the first time — no re-drilling, no leaks.

Engineered to spec

Machined mounting faces keep your pump and motor true. A generous return diffuser and dwell volume drop the oil temperature and let air separate before it reaches the suction.

  • L-shaped, overhead or standard footprint layouts
  • Machined pump & motor mounting pads
  • Return diffuser and de-aeration baffling
  • Cleanout covers, breather, filler-strainer
  • Level/temperature gauge and drain valve
  • Sized to your flow rate and cooling load

Where they run

Machine tools, presses, test rigs, marine and industrial automation — wherever a packaged hydraulic power unit lives.
